I'm a **human-centred designer dedicated to creating meaningful, impactful experiences** that redefine how people engage with products and services.

My work spans the full arc of a product — facilitating research and workshops, shaping strategy, and designing the interactions and interfaces that bring it to life. I care as much about **why** something should be built as I do about the craft of building it well.

Working as a consultant across banking, government, superannuation, and education has taught me to design within real constraints — commercial goals, compliance, and delivery timelines — without losing sight of the people on the other side of the screen.
